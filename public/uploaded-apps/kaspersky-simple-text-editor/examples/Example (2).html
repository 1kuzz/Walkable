Recertification 2026: Streamlined, automated, and expanded

CRM

KORM Resellers

We are happy to announce that the Partner Recertification process has been completed. The levels and competencies will be applied as per the approved exception requests and recommended levels.

This year's recertification introduced several key enhancements:

  • Automated product specialization assignment: Recommended Product Specialization levels are now system-calculated and assigned based on predefined requirements, streamlining the assignment and renewal process.
  • B2C partner recertification: B2C resellers in Europe now participate in the process, with access to Retail Partner and Retail Premier Partner statuses.

If you were lucky enough to participate in the updated process, please share your feedback on the partner recertification process using this survey.

02

Improved Joint Business Plan process

Partner Portal

The Joint Business Plan (JBP) process has been redesigned to be more structured, transparent, and efficient.

Plan submission and approval

  • The submission form is now more structured, with content automatically tailored to the partner type (Reseller/Distributor) and status, requesting only relevant information.
  • Partners can track their plan in real-time. If rejected, they see the rejection reason and can edit and resubmit it without completing a new form.
  • The approval process now can involve multiple approvers. The system ensures plans follow the correct approval path based on partner type and status.

Joint Business Plan dashboard

The new JBP dashboard consolidates partner-submitted forms and key partner analytics into a single tool. This streamlines the review process by giving approvers access to all necessary data, eliminating the need to consult separate reports.

03

General Terms and Conditions on the Partner Portal

Partner Portal

All separate Terms and Conditions documents are now consolidated into a single unified General T&Cs. Partners now only need to review and agree to one comprehensive document, which includes all key terms previously found in multiple agreements. 

The updated document is available on the Partner Portal:

  • Existing partners will be required to accept the updated T&Cs upon their next login to the Partner Portal.
  • New partners will review and accept the updated T&Cs as part of the standard registration form.

New features for the Named Account List (NAL)

CRM

Starting January 13, the following updates are released in Bitrix to improve the NAL process:

  • Current NAL field is added on Customer accounts to show whether there is any Draft/Awaiting Approval/Approved or Active NAL as per the date of view. The field is filled in automatically, and the filter is available in Accounts, Opportunities and Orders sections.
  • The Hierarchy tab is added on Accounts to facilitate the checks of related companies under the same Ultimate Parent Account.
  • When the account is added in Draft NAL, the owner of this account and its children will be synced with the NAL Owner.
  • To avoid mistakes when creating and submitting NALs for approval, more validations are launched, such as a check whether any account from the necessary hierarchy is already in another NAL.

06

DaData autofill on the Partner Portal

DaData

Partner Portal

We have integrated DaData's system into the Partner Portal during partner registration and within Deal Reg forms for Russia, Belarus, and Kazakhstan.

Now, partners can simply enter a company name or Tax ID to select a validated entity, after which fields like legal name, address, VAT number, industry and even e-mail will be auto-populated. This enhancement ensures higher data quality and streamlines partner operations.
