New Web KORM: A year of foundation with SBO, Regional Sales, Channel and IT teams

Web KORM

2025 was a foundational year for New Web KORM, focused on the successful soft launch of the "Quote Sharing Scenario" and preparing our global user base.

2025 regional launch timeline

  • DACH: June 1
  • META: July 15
  • Europe: September 15
  • APAC: October 15
  • LATAM: November 15

Key launch metrics

  • Total orders placed from shared quotes: 1,508
  • Orders placed specifically via New Web KORM: 773

This results in a New Web KORM adoption rate of 51.25% for this period.

A huge thank you to all teams involved for your crucial role in making this launch a success.

2026 full system launch

2026 will be defined by the full-system launch and the strategic retirement of the legacy platform across all regions and business models (B2B & B2C).

K Expert Club: Exclusive community for the technical specialists

Partner Portal

Available only for RU++ partners. The new page provides partners with full transparency and a step-by-step overview of the membership journey. The content dynamically adapts depending on the partner’s current stage in the process.

On the page, partners can:

  • Review the list of mandatory training courses, complete required certifications, and track their progress directly on the page.
  • Access detailed information about the requirements and procedure for the master exam.
  • After completing all stages, click 'Join the Community'. Once the partner submits additional information, the application is sent to the Presales team for review and approval of full K Expert Club membership.

Next year, we plan to launch an expanded and enhanced version of the page for the Global region, featuring additional functionality and new opportunities.
