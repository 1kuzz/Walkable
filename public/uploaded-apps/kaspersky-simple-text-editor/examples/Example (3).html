New MDF Administrator role for Russia++

ELMA

This role has been automatically assigned to partners with a historical record of working with MDF requests, in addition to their existing Portal Administrator role. ELMA administrators can also assign this role to other specific contacts as needed.

All system notifications related to MDF will be sent only to partner contacts who have both of the following roles:

  • Partner Portal Administrator
  • MDF Administrator

This guarantees that sensitive MDF information is directed only to the relevant contacts.

Your renewals at a glance:
Enterprise Renewal Opportunities Digest

CRM

You will get Enterprise Digest with a list of renewal opportunities that require actions from the owner in order to track progress of renewal opportunities processing and timely detect any issues or delays.

  • For Opportunity Owners – KAM/EAM – monthly
    • The summary results (in USD) on open Enterprise renewal opportunities per Owner for the period under review: Overdue, Delayed, Upcoming.
    • Action required: Get in touch with the customers, mentioned in the digest, and update the renewal opportunity (move it to the next stage, if applicable).
  • For MDs, GMs and Heads of Enterprise – twice a quarter
    • The summary results (in USD) on open opportunities per Territory, and additional breakdown on overdue and delayed Renewal opportunities by territory per EAM/KAMs.

Criteria for opportunities for digest

  • Customer's country is outside Russia++ region.
  • The opportunity create date is not earlier than one year from the current date.
  • Net Booking Amount >5k USD.
  • Nodes >=1000 OR the Security Domain includes strategic products.
